Company Account Number & Credit Control

Unique customer account numbers and reliable credit-limit protection.

Odoo 19.0 Community & Enterprise LGPL-3

Know Every Customer. Control Every Exposure.

This module automatically assigns a unique account number (P00001, P00002, ...) to every credit-controlled company. It also enforces customer credit limits and prevents Sales Order confirmation when the customer's credit exposure exceeds the configured limit.

Key Features

Automatic Account Numbers

  • Sequential account numbers: P00001, P00002, ...
  • Unique account numbers enforced by the database.
  • Existing eligible companies are numbered automatically during installation.
  • Numbers are assigned when Partner Limit is enabled.
  • Contacts and companies without a credit limit remain untouched.

Strict Credit Control

  • Uses Odoo's standard Partner Limit setting.
  • Blocks Sales Order confirmation when the credit limit would be exceeded.
  • Displays a clear validation message.
  • Standard Odoo behaviour remains unchanged when Partner Limit is disabled.
  • Batch Sales Order confirmation is checked together.
