Company Account Number & Credit Control

Unique account numbers for every company and a hard credit-limit stop on Sales Orders.

Odoo 19.0 Enterprise & Community LGPL-3

Know every customer. Control every exposure.

This module gives each credit-controlled company a unique, auto-generated account number (P00001, P00002, ...) and enforces your customer credit limits strictly, so a Sales Order can no longer be confirmed once a customer's exposure goes over their limit.

🔢 Automatic Account Numbers

  • Sequential numbers P00001, P00002, ... for companies with Partner Limit enabled
  • Guaranteed unique (database constraint)
  • Existing eligible companies numbered automatically on install
  • Number assigned instantly when Partner Limit is switched on
  • Individual contacts and companies without a limit are left untouched

🛡 Strict Credit Control

  • Uses Odoo's standard Partner Limit setting
  • Blocks Sales Order confirmation when the limit would be exceeded
  • Clear error message with the full breakdown
  • Partner Limit off? Standard behaviour, nothing changes
  • Batch confirmation is checked as a group – no bypass

Screenshots

1. Automatic account number – every credit-controlled company gets a unique, read-only Account Number (here P00020).

2. Credit limit setup – enable Odoo's standard Partner Limit in the Accounting tab and enter the amount. No extra configuration screens.

3. Confirmation blocked – when the order would push the customer over their limit, confirming the Sales Order is stopped.

Set up in 3 steps

1
Install
Install the module. Existing companies with Partner Limit are numbered automatically.
2
Set a limit
On a company, enable Partner Limit and enter the credit limit. The account number appears automatically.
3
Sell safely
Orders that push the customer over the limit can't be confirmed.
