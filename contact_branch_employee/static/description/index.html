Contact Branch & Employee Management

Organise customers into Headquarters and Branches, and assign employees to the right office.

Odoo 19.0 Enterprise & Community LGPL-3

One customer, many locations – one clear structure.

Model your customers and vendors the way they really are: a Headquarter with its Branches. Assign employees and salespersons to a Headquarter or a specific Branch, and let the module keep the HQ's sales settings in sync across all branches.

🏢 Headquarter & Branch Contacts

  • Mark any company contact as a Headquarter
  • Link branch contacts to their Headquarter
  • Branches tab with kanban cards and quick-create form
  • Automatic Branch flag and branch count
  • Open any branch in its full contact form in one click

👥 Employee Assignment

  • Assign an employee to a Headquarter or a Branch
  • Branch list is filtered by the selected Headquarter
  • Link the employee to a Customer / Vendor contact
  • Assigned Company is computed automatically
  • Branch employees cannot switch Headquarter

🔄 Headquarter-to-Branch Sync

  • Change the Salesperson on the HQ and all branches follow
  • Same for Pricelist, Payment Terms and Payment Method
  • Edits made on a branch stay on that branch only
  • No manual copying across dozens of branch records

🔍 Filters & Reporting

  • Contacts: Headquarters and Branches filters
  • Group contacts by Headquarter or Company Type
  • Employees: HQ Employees and Branch Employees filters
  • Search employees by Headquarter or Branch
  • HQ and Branch columns in the employee list

Set up in 3 steps

1
Create the Headquarter
Open a company contact and tick Is Headquarter.
2
Add the Branches
Use the Branches tab (or the Headquarter field on a contact) to link branch offices.
3
Assign employees
On the employee form, pick Headquarter or Branch in Organization Assignment.

What is synchronised from the Headquarter

Salesperson Pricelist Payment Terms Payment Method

Update one of these on the Headquarter and every branch is updated. Changing a branch only affects that branch – nothing is pushed back to the Headquarter or to other branches.
