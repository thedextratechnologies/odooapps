Contact Branch & Employee Management
====================================

Organise customer and vendor contacts into Headquarters and Branches, assign
employees to a Headquarter or Branch, and keep key sales settings synchronised
from the Headquarter to all of its branches.

Configuration
=============

* No additional configuration needed
* Tick **Is Headquarter** on a company contact, then link branch contacts to it
  (from the *Branches* tab or the **Headquarter** field on the contact)
* On an employee, use the **Organization Assignment** group to choose
  Headquarter or Branch

Features
========

* Headquarter and Branch contact management
* Branches tab on the Headquarter with kanban cards and quick-create form
* Automatic "Branch" flag and branch count
* Employee assignment to a Headquarter or to a specific Branch
* Branch list filtered by the selected Headquarter
* Link an employee to a Customer / Vendor contact
* Automatic sync from Headquarter to all branches:
  Salesperson, Pricelist, Payment Terms and Payment Method
* Changes made on a branch stay on that branch only
* Validation rules: a Headquarter cannot belong to another Headquarter, a contact
  cannot be its own Headquarter, a Headquarter with branches cannot be deleted,
  and a branch must belong to the selected Headquarter
* Branch employees cannot change their assigned Headquarter
* Extra filters, search fields and group-by for contacts and employees

Compatibility
=============

* Odoo 19.0 (Enterprise and Community)
* Depends on ``base``, ``contacts``, ``hr`` and ``account``
